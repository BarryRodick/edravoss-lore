# Edravoss

Edravoss is a fantasy setting shaped by journeys into the unknown and by what
people choose to bring home. Its stories attend to individuals, communities,
and the lasting consequences of difficult choices.

This first entry establishes only that broad foundation. It establishes no
named character, place, faction, event, chronology, creature, artefact, or
system of magic. Those details become public canon only when a later entry
states them directly.
