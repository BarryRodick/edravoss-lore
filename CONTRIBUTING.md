# Contributing

Edravoss lore is in a limited public-launch phase.

## Use Discussions for ideas and feedback

Please use GitHub Discussions for high-level ideas, questions, typo reports,
and feedback. Share only material you are comfortable making public, and do not
post confidential information or material owned by somebody else.

An idea in Discussions is not canon, does not guarantee use or attribution, and
does not create permission for the project to copy a contributor's protected
expression. Until reviewed contributor terms exist, do not submit polished
story prose, character or setting designs, artwork, maps, audio, or other
creative assets.

## Pull requests are closed at launch

External pull requests are not accepted during the public-launch phase. In
particular, external story-text pull requests will not be reviewed or merged
until contributor terms have been approved and this policy has been updated.
Maintainers may still use pull requests for internal editorial work.
