# Game preview screenshots

**Status:** reviewed development screenshots; not setting canon

**Publication authority:** Barry Rodick's request of 12 September 2026 to
capture his game and publish a coming-soon preview on this website.

## Source and capture

These are authentic screenshots of *Edravoss: The Broken Road*, captured on
12 September 2026 from the game's committed development build
`3b6067f420c189b9d6f67b72b5751fbca33ca326` with Godot 4.7.1. An isolated copy
of that build was used, preserving ongoing development work.

The game's existing rendered practice journeys exercised its normal interface
and saved the viewport as PNG. The selected captures show the practice board,
the character's equipment and a resolved practice encounter. Each is
1280 by 720 pixels. The PNG files are published unchanged, with no compositing,
retouching, overlays or generated replacement imagery.

These are staged practice sessions. They do not depict an authored Mission
or promise a final game, release date or supported platform. The website labels
the preview “Coming soon” and “Work in progress”.
